Before You Report

Study & Fitness Plan

A structured week-by-week plan to prepare holistically — psychology, GTO, interview, technical, and physical fitness — plus the physical/medical standards you should already be meeting.

🗓️ 4-Week Plan 🏃 Physical Standards 📚 Recommended Resources 🎒 Packing List

1 4-Week Prep Schedule

Compress or stretch proportionally depending on how much time you have before your reporting date.

Week 1 — Foundations

OIR practice sets daily. Learn PPDT/TAT story structure and write 2 stories/day. Start daily 3–4 km runs and basic strength training. Skim through Navy general knowledge.

Week 2 — Psychology + GTO Basics

Daily WAT (60 words) & SRT (60 situations) timed sets. Learn all GTO tasks conceptually. Draft your SDT and PIQ answers. Increase run distance; add rope climbing/pull-ups.

Week 3 — Mock Practice

Full mock GD sessions with peers. Mock command task planning. Mock interview with a mentor. Revise CS fundamentals + cyber security basics 30 min/day. Continue fitness routine.

Week 4 — Consolidation

Lighter revision only — don't cram new material. Sleep well, taper physical training, finalise documents and packing, do a final relaxed mock interview.

2 Physical & Medical Standards (General Guidance)

Exact current standards are notified in each SSC IT Executive recruitment notification — always cross-check against the latest official Indian Navy notification before your batch. As general preparation targets:

  • Maintain a healthy BMI within the commonly prescribed defence range
  • Get vision, dental and ENT checked early — treat correctable issues (e.g., minor dental cavities) well in advance
  • Build running stamina (aim sub-official-cutoff timing for 1.6 km with margin), basic push-ups/sit-ups capacity
  • Avoid tattoos in non-permitted body areas as per current Navy tattoo policy
Always verify exact medical/physical standards from the official Indian Navy recruitment notification (joinindiannavy.gov.in) for your specific entry and batch — requirements can be revised.

3 Recommended Study Resources

OIR / Reasoning

Any standard SSB OIR practice book with verbal + non-verbal reasoning sets; time yourself strictly against a stopwatch.

Psychology Tests

Standard TAT/WAT/SRT/SDT SSB preparation guides for structure and sample picture sets — practice writing by hand under time pressure, not just reading theory.

GTO & Interview

SSB GTO/Interview preparation guides covering GD, GPE, PGT/HGT, Lecturette and PIQ-based interview technique.

Current Affairs

A daily newspaper or reliable news app, focused on national/defence/ science-technology sections; keep a running notes file of key facts.

Technical (CS/IT)

Standard undergraduate-level notes/short revision guides on DS&A, OS, DBMS, Networks, OOP — revision-level depth is enough, not expert depth.

Official Source

Always cross-check exam pattern, vacancies, eligibility, and standards on the official Indian Navy recruitment website before your process.
