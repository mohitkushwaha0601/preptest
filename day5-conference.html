Day 5

Conference & Final Results

The final day. All three assessors (Interviewing Officer, Group Testing Officer, Psychologist) pool their independent assessments before the full board sees you briefly. This is a review, not a new test to "perform" in.

🪖 Uniform / formal dress 🧑‍⚖️ Full board present ⏱️ ~1–2 minutes per candidate 📋 Results announced same day

1 What Happens

Assessor Conference (before you enter)

The IO, GTO and Psychologist meet independently and discuss/reconcile their observations of each candidate across all 4 days, arriving at a consensus recommendation.

Board Appearance

You're called in briefly (often one by one, sometimes with a couple of quick questions from the President/Deputy President of the board) — mostly a formality confirming what's already been assessed.

Results

Chest numbers of recommended candidates are read out. Recommended candidates proceed to the Medical Board; others receive brief, generic feedback if they ask.

2 How To Conduct Yourself

Dress & Bearing

Wear the same formal/interview attire, well-ironed, polished shoes. Stand with good posture, greet the board respectfully, maintain eye contact.

If Asked Anything

Answer briefly, honestly and confidently — there is nothing left to "prove" at this stage, so don't overthink or over-explain.

Regardless of Outcome

Note down your own honest reflections on strengths/weak spots across the week — extremely useful if you reattempt in future.

There's no separate "conference performance" to prepare for beyond being consistent with who you've been all week. This day rewards genuineness shown on Days 1–4, not last-minute impression management.

3 After Recommendation: What's Next

  1. Medical Examination at a Military Hospital (usually 3–5 days) — get any known issues (vision, dental, ENT, BMI) checked and treated well in advance.
  2. Merit List / Final Selection based on SSB marks + medical fitness, as per vacancies for SSC IT Executive that year.
  3. Joining Instructions for training at INS Chilka (basic) and subsequently INS Shivaji / CME etc. for the IT branch professional training.
