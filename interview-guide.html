Personal Interview Guide

Conducted one-on-one by the Interviewing Officer (IO), usually 30–45 minutes, on any day between Day 2 and Day 4. It's based almost entirely on your PIQ (Personal Information Questionnaire) form — so your prep starts there.

📄 PIQ-based 🕐 30–45 minutes 🗣️ Conversational, not interrogative 💻 Extra technical questions for IT Executive entry

1 The PIQ Form Is Everything

You fill the PIQ (Personal Information Questionnaire) on reporting day, covering family, education, hobbies, achievements, and self-assessment questions. Every single line you write can be asked about — so:

  • Never write a hobby, achievement or strength you can't discuss in depth for 2–3 minutes
  • Keep a copy of what you wrote (mentally or on paper) to revise before your interview slot
  • Be fully honest — inconsistency between your PIQ, interview answers and psychology test responses is the #1 red flag

2 Typical Interview Flow

Warm-up

Introduce yourself, native place, family background — an ice-breaker to ease nerves.

Education & Career

Your degree, subjects, projects, why IT/CS, academic performance, favourite subjects.

Why Navy / Why IT Executive

Motivation, awareness of the role, why Navy over other services/private jobs.

Hobbies & Extra-curricular

Depth questions on whatever you listed — sports, reading, coding projects, NCC, etc.

General Awareness

Current affairs, defence news, basic geography/history, and technical/IT questions.

Situational / Stress Questions

Hypotheticals or mild stress questions to test composure, honesty and judgement.

3 Likely Question Bank

  • Tell me about yourself in 2 minutes.
  • Tell me about your family — what do your parents/siblings do?
  • What values did your parents instill in you?
  • Describe your daily routine.
  • What are your strengths and weaknesses? Give real examples.
  • Who is your role model and why?
  • What do your friends say about you?
  • Explain your final year project in simple terms.
  • What programming languages/tools are you strongest in?
  • Why did you choose Computer Science / IT?
  • What was your toughest subject and how did you manage it?
  • Any internships or certifications — what did you learn?
  • Difference between your degree's focus and what IT Executive role needs — how will you bridge it?
  • Why do you want to join the Indian Navy specifically (not Army/Air Force)?
  • Why SSC (Short Service Commission) and not a permanent commission route?
  • What do you know about the role of an IT Executive Officer in the Navy?
  • What ranks/insignia do Navy officers have? (Sub Lieutenant → Lieutenant → Lt. Commander...)
  • Name some Naval bases/commands in India (Western, Eastern, Southern Naval Command).
  • What will you do if you're not recommended this time?
  • Are you willing to serve anywhere in India, including remote or sea-going postings?
  • Recent Indian Navy inductions (INS ships, submarines, indigenous aircraft carrier, etc.)
  • India's stance on regional maritime security (Indian Ocean Region, String of Pearls).
  • Recent government initiatives: Digital India, Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence manufacturing.
  • Major recent cyber security incidents/policies in India.
  • Current Chief of Naval Staff, Defence Minister, key defence exercises (Malabar, Milan, Varuna).
  • If your senior gives an order you believe is wrong, what will you do?
  • You made a mistake in a report at work — how do you handle it?
  • How do you handle criticism from a superior in front of others?
  • Would you follow an order you personally disagree with?
  • Describe a time you failed and what you learned.

4 Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do

Be truthful even about failures. Speak in simple, clear sentences. Admit "I don't know" rather than bluffing on facts. Maintain calm eye contact and natural body language.

🚫 Don't

Don't rehearse robotic scripted answers word-for-word. Don't badmouth family, teachers or past employers. Don't argue with the IO if challenged — explain your view calmly instead.
