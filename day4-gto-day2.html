Day 4

GTO Tasks — Day II

The most individually-visible GTO day: Command Task puts you solely in charge of 2–3 subordinates, Individual Obstacles test raw physical drive, and the Final Group Task wraps up team assessment.

👑 Command Task 🏃 Individual Obstacles (10) 🐍 Snake Race / Group Obstacle Race 🧗 Final Group Task (FGT)

1 Command Task

Each candidate is given an obstacle (similar style to PGT/HGT) to solve as the "commander," selecting 2–3 "subordinates" from the rest of the group to help — but you must personally lead and do the bulk of the physical work, not just direct others.

  • Choose subordinates who are cooperative/physically capable, not just friends
  • Explain your plan clearly, assign specific roles, and stay hands-on throughout
  • If your first plan fails, adapt calmly rather than freezing — GTO wants to see problem-solving under pressure

2 Individual Obstacles (IO) — 10 Obstacles

A timed circuit of ~10 physical obstacles (each worth different points), completed individually in ~3 minutes. Total possible score matters, but so does technique/confidence shown while attempting harder ones.

ObstaclePointsNote
Balancing Log2Walk across without falling
Tarzan Swing4Swing across using a rope
Double Fence5Cross two parallel fences
Wall Climb6Climb over the vertical wall
Tiger Leap10Dive/jump through a tyre onto a mat, highest scoring
You don't need to complete all 10 — attempting the higher-value ones with correct technique and genuine effort (even if imperfect) shows courage and stamina, two directly assessed OLQs.

3 Snake Race / Group Obstacle Race

Teams are split into two groups that race through a chain of obstacles connected by a rope ("snake"), combining speed with coordinated teamwork. Encourage teammates, keep pace as a unit, don't drag the rope carelessly.

4 Final Group Task (FGT)

The last group obstacle task, usually a slightly easier/shorter version combining elements from earlier tasks — the GTO's final chance to observe team cohesion and whether earlier lessons/roles have been absorbed by the group as a whole.

5 Cross-Cutting GTO Tips

Physical Prep

Build stamina (3–5 km jog), grip strength (rope climbing), core strength (plank, sit-ups) and basic flexibility weeks in advance — outdoor tasks run for hours in the sun.

Mental Prep

GTO tasks are group efforts — you are scored on contribution & cooperation, not on solo heroics or on your idea "winning." Support good ideas even if they aren't yours.

Safety First

Never take reckless risks to "look brave" — GTOs stop unsafe attempts immediately and it reflects poorly on judgement, one of the core assessed traits.
