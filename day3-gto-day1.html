Day 3

GTO Tasks — Day I

The Group Testing Officer (GTO) observes you in outdoor, physical, team-based tasks over Days 3–4. Day 1 of GTO typically covers: GTO's introductory talk, Group Discussion, Group Planning Exercise, Progressive Group Task and the first Half Group Task, plus your Lecturette.

🗣️ GD (2 topics) 🗺️ GPE (military map task) 🧗 PGT (4 obstacles) 🎤 Lecturette 🧗 HGT

1 GTO's Opening Talk

The GTO briefs the group about the entire GTO series of tasks, sets expectations of team spirit and discipline, and takes note of your general demeanour from the very first minute. First impressions matter here too.

2 Group Discussion (GD)

Usually 2 topics — often one social/current-affairs topic and one on a general theme. ~10–15 minutes each.

  • Prepare a mental structure: define the topic → causes/context → both sides of the argument → practical way forward
  • Reference current national/defence/tech topics confidently but don't fake expertise
  • Balance airtime — speak substantively 4–6 times, don't monopolise or stay silent

3 Group Planning Exercise (GPE) / Military Plan

A model/map of a fictitious area with a narrated situation (crisis: fire, flood, accident, sabotage) containing several problems (rescue, resources, hazards, distances, terrain). Steps:

  1. Reading (5 min): narrator reads the problem twice — note down all resources, hazards, distances, times given.
  2. Individual solution (10 min): write your own plan solving every sub-problem using only the stated resources.
  3. Group discussion (20 min): group discusses and agrees on one common plan.
  4. Announcement: one nominated candidate presents the final group solution to the GTO.
Address every problem mentioned in the narration — missing even one sub-problem is heavily penalised. Prioritise saving human life first, then resources, using the exact resources given (no assumptions).

4 Progressive Group Task (PGT)

A series of 4 progressively harder obstacles, each with a "load" (a heavy structure/helmet representing a fictitious important object) that the whole group must carry across using ropes, planks, drums and a set of rules (out-of-bound areas, no touching ground within certain zones, load must be carried by hand not thrown, etc.).

  • Listen carefully to all rules before starting — rule violations invalidate your team's attempt
  • Contribute ideas early, help physically, and don't disappear into the background once your idea is picked or rejected
  • Support teammates crossing obstacles; safety and team success outrank personal credit

5 Half Group Task (HGT)

Same style as PGT but with half the group (easier logistics, but you get less "cover" — your individual contribution is more visible). Same rules and mindset apply.

6 Lecturette (2–3 minutes)

You pick one of 4 topic cards (usually one social, one abstract/philosophical, one on current affairs/defence, one technical/sports) and speak for 2–3 minutes with 3 minutes prior preparation time on a scrap paper.

  • Structure: brief intro/definition → 2–3 solid points with examples → concise conclusion
  • Practice a "default structure" you can apply to almost any topic beforehand
  • Prepare especially well on: cyber security, digital India, AI/emerging tech, Indian Navy modernisation, and general current affairs — natural strengths for an IT Executive candidate
