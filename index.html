i Overall SSB Process

The Naval SSB (like all Services Selection Boards) runs over 5 days at a Selection Centre. Day 1 screens you out or in; Days 2–4 (Testing Days) run Psychology tests, GTO outdoor tasks and the Personal Interview in parallel; Day 5 is the Conference where the final board decision is announced.

DAY 0

Reporting & Documentation

Arrival at the Selection Centre, document verification, joining instructions, chest number allotment, briefing.

DAY 1 · SCREENING

OIR Test + PPDT

Verbal & Non-Verbal reasoning test followed by Picture Perception & Discussion Test. Results decide who stays for the remaining 4 days.

DAY 2 · PSYCHOLOGY

TAT, WAT, SRT, SDT

Four projective psychology tests that reveal your personality, values and Officer Like Qualities (OLQs) through spontaneous writing.

DAY 3 · GTO DAY I

GD, GPE, PGT, HGT, Lecturette

Outdoor Group Testing Officer tasks begin — group discussion, planning exercise and physical/mental group tasks. Interviews also run in parallel.

DAY 4 · GTO DAY II

Command Task, Individual Obstacles, Snake Race, FGT

Individual leadership and physical tasks. Interviews continue for candidates not yet covered.

DAY 5 · CONFERENCE

Board Conference & Results

Each candidate is called before the full board briefly, then final recommended/not recommended results are announced.

3 The 15 Officer Like Qualities (OLQs)

Every test across all 5 days — OIR, PPDT, psychology, GTO, interview — is silently measuring these same qualities. Keep them in mind constantly.

Factor I — Planning & Organising

Effective Intelligence, Reasoning, Organising Ability, Power of Expression

Factor II — Social Adjustment

Social Adaptability, Cooperation, Sense of Responsibility

Factor III — Social Effectiveness

Initiative, Self Confidence, Speed of Decision, Ability to Influence the Group, Liveliness

Factor IV — Dynamic / Achievement

Determination, Courage, Stamina
